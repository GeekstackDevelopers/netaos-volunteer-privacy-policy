Privacy Policy

NetaOS Field · Effective date: 21 October 2026

1. About this policy

NetaOS Field (“the App”, “we”, “us”) is a mobile application used by authorised volunteers to conduct field surveys. This policy explains what information the App collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

2. Who can use the App

The App is available only to volunteers whose accounts have been created by an administrator. You cannot self-register. If you need an account, you can request one, and an administrator will review the request and create the account if approved.

3. Information we collect

  • Phone number. Used to create your account and to sign you in with a one-time password (OTP) sent by SMS.
  • Account details. Information provided in your account request or by the administrator, such as your name and assigned role or area.
  • Location data. Your device location is collected only while you are actively carrying out a survey, to verify that survey responses were recorded in the assigned area.
  • Survey data. The responses, notes and related details you record during a survey, along with timestamps.
  • Technical data. Basic device and app information (such as app version and error logs) needed to keep the App working and secure.

We do not collect your location when you are not in an active survey, and the App does not track you in the background outside of survey sessions.

4. How we use information

  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To collect, store and process survey responses.
  • To verify the place and time of survey submissions and ensure data quality.
  • To let administrators manage accounts, assignments and survey progress.
  • To diagnose problems and improve the App.

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for advertising.

5. Location permission

The App asks for permission to access your device location. You can allow or deny this in your device settings at any time. Without location access you may not be able to start or submit surveys, because location verification is part of the survey process.

6. Sharing of information

Information is accessible to authorised administrators of the organisation running the survey. We may share data with service providers that help us operate the App (for example, hosting and SMS delivery for OTPs), and only as needed for those services. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users and others.

7. Data storage and security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. Sign-in credentials on your device are kept in the platform’s secure storage. Access to survey and account data is restricted to authorised personnel. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information.

8. Data retention

We keep account information while your account is active, and survey and location records for as long as needed for the survey programme and related reporting or legal purposes. After that, data is deleted or anonymised.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off location access in your device settings.
  • Ask to access, correct or delete your personal information.
  • Ask for your account to be deactivated.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. Some survey data may be retained where needed for the integrity of the survey results or to meet legal obligations.

10. Children

The App is intended for adults. We do not knowingly create accounts for anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last revised. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact us

For questions or requests about this policy, email geekstackdevelopment@gmail.com.
